Network:

* The Logical Topology (also known as the signal or data flow topology) describes how the data actually flows through the network, independent of the physical arrangement of the devices.



* The Physical Topology is the actual, visible, and tangible layout of a network



Type of Physical Topology: 

* BUS Topology	All devices are connected to a single main cable (backbone) that runs between two endpoints.
* STAR Topology All devices are connected individually to a central connecting device, like a switch or hub. (Most common today).
* RING Topology Devices are connected point-to-point in a closed loop, where each device connects to exactly two others.
* MESH Topology Every device is connected directly to every other device on the network, providing high redundancy.
* TREE Topology A hybrid topology that connects multiple STAR networks using a central BUS backbone.
