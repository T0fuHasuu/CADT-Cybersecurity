3. Key Security Risk & Threats

We always take CIA triad as a check list

- risk 1 (ransomware) : outdated laptop or small mistake can lead to data being compromised 
- risk 2 (data exfiltration) : employee copy and access to sensitive information 
- risk 3 (lateral movement) : 1 infected device can lead to whole organization shut down
- risk 4 (physical theft) : a stolen laptop can be a bad news if it doesn't have FDE

[] Unmanaged device will be the biggest risk in the company
[] Patch gap can be watch closely by the attacker and will be damaged if not fix quickly

4. Tool open source stack

- WAZUH : detect real time and respond
- Tactical RMM : reports and update gap to help mitigate risks
- ClamAV & MCS-Defender : To provide layer malware protection 
- Using the Default Deny
- Whitelisting : AppLocker Policy so that only a specific role can access to that to info
